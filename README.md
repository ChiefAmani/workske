# All Clean Property Services - Marketing & Operations System

## Repository Purpose
This repository contains the technical specifications, research findings, and operational tools for **All Clean Property Services**, a window and gutter cleaning company. The primary focus is on developing a retargeting strategy using email and SMS marketing to re-engage past clients and increase recurring revenue. Additionally, it houses financial models and business strategies for operational tools like the Profit Per Job Calculator.

## Key Findings: Marketing System (Build vs. Buy)
To address the low answer rate (20%) of manual phone calls, we researched implementing an automated Email and SMS marketing system. 

**Recommendation: BUY (Outsource to existing platforms)**
*   **Efficiency:** Leveraging established platforms (e.g., Mailchimp, SimpleTexting) allows for rapid deployment and immediate ROI.
*   **Compliance:** Third-party tools automatically handle complex legal requirements like CAN-SPAM, GDPR, and TCPA.
*   **Cost-Effectiveness:** The subscription costs of existing platforms are significantly lower than the development, maintenance, and infrastructure costs required to build a custom solution from scratch.
*   **Focus:** Outsourcing allows the business to focus on its core operations rather than software development.
