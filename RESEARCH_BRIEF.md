PROJECT: Email and Text Marketing System for All Clean Property Services

RESEARCH QUESTIONS:
1. What are the leading email marketing platforms suitable for small businesses, considering features and pricing?
2. What are the leading text marketing platforms suitable for small businesses, considering features and pricing?
3. What are the key advantages and disadvantages of developing an in-house email/text marketing system?
4. What are the key advantages and disadvantages of outsourcing email/text marketing to existing platforms?
5. What is the 3-year Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) and projected ROI for building in-house versus buying a SaaS solution?
6. Based on the comparison, which approach (in-house vs. outsourced) is recommended for All Clean Property Services to achieve recurring revenue through retargeting old clients?

METHODOLOGY:
1. Conduct internet searches for "email marketing platforms for small business comparison", "text marketing platforms for small business comparison", and "in-house marketing automation vs outsourced pros cons".
2. Analyze search results to identify prominent platforms, their core features, and general pricing models.
3. Extract common pros and cons associated with both in-house development and outsourcing of marketing automation systems.
4. Model financial scenarios to calculate TCO and ROI based on standard developer rates and SaaS pricing tiers.
5. Synthesize findings to address the research questions and formulate a recommendation.

SOURCES TO INVESTIGATE:
- Industry comparison websites for email marketing platforms (e.g., Brevo, Mailchimp, Constant Contact, HubSpot, Omnisend).
- Industry comparison websites for text marketing platforms (e.g., HubSpot, Customer.io, Realize).
- Articles and whitepapers discussing the strategic decision between in-house and outsourced marketing technology.
- Pricing pages for SaaS platforms to build the TCO model.

ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK:
For each option (in-house vs. outsourced):
- Features: What functionalities are typically offered?
- Cost & ROI: What is the TCO over 3 years, and what is the projected ROI?
- Control & Customization: How much control and customization is possible?
- Scalability: How well does it scale with business growth?
- Time & Resources: What are the time and resource commitments?
- Integration: How easily does it integrate with existing systems (e.g., current booking/invoicing site)?
- Maintenance & Support: What level of ongoing maintenance and support is required/provided?
- Security & Data Privacy: Considerations for client data.
